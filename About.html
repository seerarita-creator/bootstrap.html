About me

Profile summary

My name is Seera Rita Materesi. Iam a female, old enough to make personal decisions about my life. I come from the eastern part of uganda, Mbale city in particular which makes me a mugisu by tribe. I currently live at Kyengera-Wakiso. My educational journey started way back in 2000, at elgon nursery and primary school-Mbale, and its at this school that i completed my primary seven. I went to lakeside college luzira for my senior one and two,but later on moved to merryland high school-entebbe where i completed my senior four and six exams. I was blessed to join medical school for a diploma in pharmacy at UIAHMS-mulago, and here i qualified as a pharmacy technician/dispenser. I worked for afew years with two different institutions, that is first pharmacy and Uganda martyrs hospital lubaga. While working with these institutions, most of my work was done using systems that i happened to pick interest in as a new found dream. with my new found interest, i pushed for admission into an institution that would let me become a system designer, and thats how i found my way to Bugema University. I am currently in my first year of the course.
